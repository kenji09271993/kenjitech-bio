01

Medical Commercialization

Inside Sales BPO — Japan

Great medical technologies often never reach the clinics that need them. We connect healthtech companies with clinics and hospitals across Japan and design the path to adoption as a system — recording every voice from the field to build a deep understanding of medicine’s real problems.

Our model is an inside-sales BPO specialized in healthcare: from target design to outreach and booking meetings, end to end. We currently serve the Japanese market.

02

99.9 (Three Nines)

Performance Nutrition

Redesigning energy during exercise through science. Built around high-purity fructose, we are developing drinks and apparel for athletes — and only what has been verified with research institutions becomes a product.

Research
ConceptResearchDevelopmentActive

Joint research with a research institution is in preparation (planned for 2027). Details will be published once intellectual property is protected.

Long-term goal

20%

Replace 20% of the global sugar market with fructose products.

This is a long-term goal, not a current result or forecast. Global sugar consumption is about 188.79 million tonnes per year (2025/26 forecast, raw value)[1]. 20% equals roughly 37.76 million tonnes (simple volume-based calculation).

05 / Founder

Kenji Nagae

Founder

Number one,
or nothing.

It all began with the frustration of a body that would not move the way I wanted. After asking “why” again and again, what I found was not a lack of ability, but a version of myself that had let go of the pride to get back up the moment I fell.

So I made getting back up immediately, no matter how many times I fall, the standard for myself and for this company. Knowledge, capital and technology take no shape without a vessel to hold them. That vessel is strength of mind.

If the vessel that welcomes people of a number-one standard does not hold a number-one standard itself, no one will follow. That is why this company holds itself to number one, and by that standard brings science into everyday life — to be Super Luck for everyone we meet.
