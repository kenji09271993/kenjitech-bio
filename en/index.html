Medical Commercialization

Inside Sales BPO — Japan

Great medical technologies often never reach the clinics that need them. We connect healthtech companies with clinics and hospitals across Japan and design the path to adoption as a system — recording every voice from the field to build a deep understanding of medicine’s real problems.

Our model is an inside-sales BPO specialized in healthcare: from target design to outreach and booking meetings, end to end. We currently serve the Japanese market.

04 / Technology

YATA — In-house design platform

YATA is the design platform Kenjitech Bio builds in-house. Our founder had long felt the need for real design capability. A broken arm made that need personal, and YATA was born.

Even in high-pressure settings, anyone can design intuitively, down to the millimeter. Intuitive and simple: that is the core of its design.

06 / Founder

Kenji Nagae

Founder

Number one,
or nothing.

It all began with the frustration of a body that would not move the way I wanted. After asking “why” again and again, what I found was not a lack of ability, but a version of myself that had let go of the pride to get back up the moment I fell.

“Number one, or nothing” is a standard I hold myself to. It is not one I impose on others.

The higher the standard, the more it matters that people can speak freely. Unless mistakes and disagreement can be raised right away, no high standard can be reached. If something is on your mind, reach out — titles don’t matter.

08 / FAQ

FAQ

Is YATA a medical device?

No. It is a design platform for research and development, and is not used for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Can I use or buy YATA?

It is currently used for our own research and development. For partnerships or joint development, please contact us.

Who is the medical commercialization service for?

Healthtech companies that want to bring their products or services to medical institutions. In the first meeting we work out which institutions to target.

Can I talk to you about investing?

We explain our business plan and research progress in a meeting. This website is not a solicitation to acquire securities.

Will you publish research results?

Yes — good or bad — once the related intellectual property is protected.

I found an error or disagree with something on this site.

Please tell us through the contact form. We correct errors and keep a record of the date and content of each correction.

Beware of impersonation. We only contact you from abe9161@gmail.com. We never ask for investment or money transfers via social media direct messages.
